# New Zealand AI Strategy — “Investing with Confidence”

**Source**  
Ministry of Business, Innovation & Employment (MBIE)  
https://www.mbie.govt.nz/business-and-employment/economic-growth/digital-policy/new-zealands-ai-strategy-investing-with-confidence  

**Published**  
July 2025 (web version maintained)

**Nature**  
Government strategy focused on accelerating private-sector adoption of existing AI technologies, responsible use, and building confidence. Light-touch; does not create new legal obligations.

---

## Key Themes That Align with CAT

| Strategy Theme | CAT / Aether Response |
|----------------|-----------------------|
| Accelerate responsible private-sector adoption | Production-oriented skills, HITL gates, measurement of real ROI (CPST) |
| Build trust and confidence | Fail-closed defaults, audit trails, explicit human authority |
| Use existing legal frameworks (Privacy Act, etc.) | Hardened into mandatory controls rather than voluntary guidance |
| International engagement on responsible AI | Alignment with Algorithm Charter + Te Mana Raraunga as distinct NZ contribution |
| Focus on application rather than foundational model development | Matches CAT’s edge + agentic application focus (not training frontier models) |
