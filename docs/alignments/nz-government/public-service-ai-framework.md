# Public Service AI Framework & GenAI Guidance

**Source**  
Government Chief Digital Officer (GCDO)  
Public Service AI Framework + Responsible AI Guidance for the Public Service (GenAI)

**Nature**  
Non-binding guidance for public-service agencies. Encourages governance, meaningful human supervision, and accountability for AI-assisted decisions.

---

## Alignment Points

| Guidance Theme | CAT Response |
|----------------|--------------|
| Meaningful human supervision | HITL L2+ as default for production / high-impact actions; agents inform and draft only |
| Accountability for decisions | Named ownership, audit trails, decision artefacts |
| Risk-based approach | Explicit risk tiering (Low → Critical) mapped to gate levels |
| Transparency | Structured logging and human-readable audit views |
| Avoid over-reliance on AI output | Fail-closed + grounding / verification requirements |

---

## Reinforcement

- Official GCDO / Public Service AI materials (primary)
- Cross-reference with Algorithm Charter and Hardened NZ AI Safety Guidelines

## Status in CAT

Treated as baseline expectations that CAT hardens into mandatory, fail-closed controls for all systems under the stack (edge, hybrid, agentic).
