# Kaitiaki Grant Lifecycle

Full funding lifecycle support for NZ grants under Te Mana Raraunga and hard HITL. Complements `grants-agent`.

## When to Load

- Discovering or prioritising grant opportunities
- Positioning CAT / Mana Kai / Hub projects for funders
- Drafting proposals or responses
- Administering contracts or reporting
- TPK, He Ara, MPI, MBIE, regional, or iwi-linked funding pathways

## Lifecycle Stages

1. **Discover** — open calls, fit scoring, eligibility
2. **Position** — narrative alignment, evidence, claim-tier control
3. **Draft** — proposal sections under HITL
4. **Submit** — human only; agent never submits
5. **Administer** — reporting, variations, relationship care

## Non-Negotiables

- No submission without explicit founder approval
- No invented partnerships, iwi endorsement, or traction
- Te Mana Raraunga and cultural review for whenua/iwi-linked narratives
- Claim tier L1 Designed until evidence supports higher
